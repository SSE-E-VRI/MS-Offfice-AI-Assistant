ISSUE:
------
Add-in fails to load on Office 2021 and machines with prior builds installed.
Ribbon does not appear; LoadBehavior is demoted to 2 after activation failure.

ROOT CAUSE:
-----------
Stale COM class registration from older build versions. Orphaned version
subkeys (1.0.0.0) point to obsolete assembly identities. The COM activation
engine selects the HIGHEST version, causing manifest-mismatch (0x80131040).

THIS WAS NOT A VERSION-COMPATIBILITY BUG. It was a REGISTRATION-CLEANUP BUG
affecting any machine with prior builds installed.
